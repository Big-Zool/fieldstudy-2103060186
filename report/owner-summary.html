Website review for Final International University

Prepared by Abdalla Ali (2103060186) · 10 November 2026 · About www.final.edu.tr

I looked at your website the way a visitor would, on a normal computer and on a slow mobile-style connection. Here is what I found, in plain words.

What is working well

  • It is safe to visit. Every page opens with the padlock (a secure, private connection), even if someone types the old “http” address.
  • Text appears quickly. The words on each page arrive first, so even on a slow phone connection people can start reading the news and menus within a few seconds.

The three things to fix first

1. The home page is very heavy — 24 MB, like downloading five songs.

What visitors experience: on a slow phone connection the home page was still loading after a full minute. Most of the weight is two news photos of almost 8 MB each, shown as tiny pictures the size of a stamp.

Why it matters: future students, often abroad and on mobile data, may give up before the page finishes, and it uses up their data allowance.

Ask your web developer to: “shrink the news thumbnails and slider photos to the size they are shown on screen, and only load pictures when the visitor scrolls to them.”

How to check it is fixed: right-click a small news picture and choose “Open image in new tab”. It should open as a small picture, not a huge photo.

2. Some visitors cannot use the menus or the contact form.

What visitors experience: people who use the keyboard instead of a mouse cannot open the “Hakkımızda”, “Akademik”, “Öğrenciler” and “Kampüs” menus at all. People who are blind and use a screen-reading program hear the contact form boxes without their names (“Name”, “E-mail”…), and many links are read out with no name.

Why it matters: a university should be open to students with disabilities, and these fixes also help Google understand the site.

Ask your web developer to: “make the site pass an accessibility check: connect each form box to its name, let the menus open with the keyboard, and give every icon and picture a short description.”

How to check it is fixed: click on the word “İsim” in the contact form — the typing cursor should jump into the box. Then put the mouse away and press the Tab key: you should be able to reach every menu.

3. Visitors who take a wrong turn are left with nothing.

What visitors experience: an old or mistyped link shows a completely white page, with no logo, menu or message. A search that finds nothing shows only the word “Sonuç”. A search of one or two letters does nothing at all, with no explanation.

Why it matters: people think the site is broken and leave, instead of finding what they came for.

Ask your web developer to: “add a friendly ‘page not found’ page with the menu and a search box, and show clear messages when a search finds nothing or is too short.”

How to check it is fixed: type www.final.edu.tr/test-page-123 into the browser — you should see a helpful page, not a white screen. Then search for “zzzqqq” — you should see a message suggesting what to try next.

Thank you for providing a website that so many students rely on — with these three changes it will be faster, friendlier and open to everyone.
