What final.edu.tr could learn from emu.edu.tr

1. EMU shows that the basics search engines and screen readers need — a real description, a language tag and one main heading — are small additions to a page template; together with alt text and named links they are why EMU scores 89 against Final’s 63.

2. A missing page should answer with a real 404 status and at least a sentence of explanation, as EMU does; Final can go one step further by adding its menu and search box, which neither site has today.

3. An empty search should keep the visitor’s word and give a clear next step: EMU already keeps the word, and a plain “No results for ‘zzzqqq’ — try a shorter word” would beat both sites.
